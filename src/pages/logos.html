Logo variations

Seven ways to cut a new VIA from the same three letters (option B), and three ways to carry VIA's existing gold mark into the poster world (option A). Every B concept uses the same straight-cut letters plus one device from VIA's own kitchen or street.

Sole

B1 · Sun

A saffron sun rises inside the A: the fire under the spit, the start of an evening on Shrewsbury Street. The mark already running on the site.

Best for
Primary mark: site header, signage, menus, social.
Watch out
Under 32px tall the sun gets tiny; switch to Monogramma there.
SVG files
ink · paper · on-mattone · mono
{{logo:spiedo:inchiostro:mattone:-}}

Spiedo

B2 · The spit

The spit runs through all three letters and a saffron disc turns on the I. Of all seven, the most VIA-specific: it names the kitchen's signature.

Best for
Alternate primary if VIA wants the spit to lead; PigFest and menus.
Watch out
The cut needs about 48px of height to read. In one colour the disc disappears into the I.
SVG files
ink · paper · on-zafferano · mono
{{logo:arco:paper:mattone:-}}

Arco

B3 · Sunrise

The letters stand in front of a half-sun, like the light at the end of a street. Warm and bold at poster scale.

Best for
Posters, the patio, outdoor banners, the lead poster.
Watch out
Depends on the sun colour; the weakest of the set in one colour and small sizes.
SVG files
ink · paper · on-prugna · on-oliva
{{logo:targa:inchiostro:mattone:bianco}}

Targa

B4 · Street plate

An Italian street plate. VIA means street, and the plate names it, the way the corner of Shrewsbury Street would in Rome.

Best for
The building sign, the front door, menu covers, the brand book cover.
Watch out
The most literal of the seven. The frame adds width and stops it working small.
SVG files
classic · night · mono
{{logo:tondo:paper:zafferano:mattone}}

Tondo

B5 · Round stamp

Plate, sun and letters in one disc. A secondary mark that fits anywhere round: stickers, wax seals, avatars.

Best for
Takeout-bag stickers, social avatar, app icon, coasters, the gift card.
Watch out
A companion, not a replacement: the letters are small inside the disc.
SVG files
mattone · oliva · prugna · ink
{{logo:biglietto:inchiostro:mattone:zafferano}}

Biglietto

B6 · Ticket

The wordmark as its own little poster, tear-off tabs and all. It carries the site's signature interaction into print.

Best for
Gift cards, event tickets, a PigFest passport, loyalty cards.
Watch out
Illustrative: too busy for the masthead or anything under 80px wide.
SVG files
zafferano · mattone · oliva
{{logo:monogramma:inchiostro:mattone:-}}

Mono­gramma

B7 · Monogram

Just the V, with the sun dropped into its valley. The smallest form of the family.

Best for
Favicon, app icon, embroidery on aprons and napkins, the wax seal.
Watch out
Needs the full wordmark nearby the first time someone meets it.
SVG files
ink · on-mattone · on-zafferano
