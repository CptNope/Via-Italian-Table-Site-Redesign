## Direction contract

THESIS: VIA's site is an Italian billposting wall (affissioni): every offer is a dated, colour-drenched poster pasted on the wall and pasted over when its date passes. Refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed food photo with a gold serif and a "Reserve" button.

OWN-WORLD: Mid-century Italian poster craft (Depero, Olivetti, Pirelli): flat drenched fields of mattone brick red, oliva, zafferano saffron and Super Tuscan prugna plum, with ink near-black and bianco paper only for set text. Cut-paper geometry (half-sun, plate disc, wedge, stripe) as authored shapes, slight paste rotation, overlapping edges, paste-line seams. Archivo across widths: expanded black for posters, condensed for dates and prices, regular for reading.

STORY: Visitor sees what's on at VIA this week, believes it's a lively chef's kitchen with real dates and real food, and books, orders or plans an event in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed mattone lead poster. Left: stacked VIA wordmark at poster scale, kicker "Italian with a modern twist · 89 Shrewsbury Street", live open/closed status from published hours. Lower-left: working booking strip (party, date, "Find a table" to Resy; no time field because Resy's link takes only date and party size and the guest picks the time there) plus two sibling doors (Order takeout · Delivery) and (Plan an event). Right: saffron half-sun and plum plate cut-paper composition with a pasted photo slot (open kitchen / spit). Bottom edge: the next posters' tops peek in, already pasted.

FORM: Affissioni poster wall, position 3 of 7 on my ordered list (1 street plate, 2 gold-leaf shop glass, 3 poster wall, 4 Solari board, 5 Worcester lunch-car enamel, 6 gelato shop, 7 bar matchbooks). Seed key 30dd3ece (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature interaction: tear-off tabs on each poster (like Italian street flyers) carry the action and tear off on press before navigating; posters expire by date and get pasted over.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
